Emergency AC Repair in Houston: What Love Air Checks on a No-Cool Call

When your air conditioning system stops working in the middle of a sweltering Houston summer, it can be more than just uncomfortable—it can be an emergency. Love Air Conditioning Company understands the urgency of a no-cool call and is ready to provide swift and effective solutions. Here's what our skilled technicians check during an emergency AC repair in Houston.

Initial System Inspection

Upon arriving at your home, our technician will perform a comprehensive inspection of your AC system. This initial check helps us quickly diagnose the issue and determine the necessary repairs. We look for any obvious signs of damage or failure, such as leaks, unusual sounds, or component wear.

Thermostat Settings

The first step is to ensure your thermostat is set correctly. Sometimes, a simple adjustment can resolve the issue. We verify that the thermostat is on the correct setting and functioning properly.

Electrical Connections

We carefully inspect all electrical connections and components. Loose or faulty wiring can cause your AC to stop working. Our technicians will ensure everything is intact and operating safely.

Refrigerant Levels

Low refrigerant levels are a common cause of cooling issues. Our team checks the refrigerant levels and looks for any signs of leaks. If necessary, we will recharge the system to restore its cooling capacity.

Component Checks

Next, we move on to a detailed examination of key components:

  • Compressor: We check the compressor for signs of damage or failure. This component is critical for cooling, and any issues here can lead to a no-cool situation.
  • Condenser and Evaporator Coils: Clean and functional coils are essential for efficient cooling. We inspect and clean these components as needed to ensure optimal performance.
  • Fans and Motors: Malfunctioning fans or motors can impede airflow and cooling. Our technicians ensure these parts are operating correctly.

Airflow and Ductwork

Proper airflow is crucial for effective cooling. We check the ductwork for any blockages or leaks that might be restricting airflow. Ensuring your ductwork is intact can significantly improve your system's efficiency.

Conclusion

At Love Air Conditioning, we prioritize your comfort and safety. Our thorough inspection process ensures that we identify and resolve any issues promptly.
