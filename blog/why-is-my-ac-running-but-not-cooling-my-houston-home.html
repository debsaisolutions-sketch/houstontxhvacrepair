Why Is My AC Running But Not Cooling My Houston Home?

It’s a scorching summer day in Houston, and your air conditioner seems to be running, but your home just isn’t cooling down. This frustrating scenario is all too common, especially in the Texas heat. Before you reach for the phone, let's explore some common reasons why your AC might be running but not cooling effectively, and when it’s time to call a professional.

Check Your Thermostat Settings

First, ensure that your thermostat is set to the correct temperature and mode. It should be set to 'cool' and the temperature set lower than the current room temperature. Sometimes, a simple adjustment can make all the difference.

Dirty or Clogged Air Filters

One of the most frequent culprits of an AC not cooling effectively is a dirty or clogged air filter. When the filter becomes blocked, airflow is restricted, causing your system to work harder and less efficiently. It's recommended to check and replace your air filters every 1-3 months, especially during the peak cooling season.

Blocked or Closed Vents

Inspect the vents in your home to ensure they are open and not obstructed by furniture or other objects. Blocked vents can prevent cool air from circulating properly, making your AC seem ineffective.

Refrigerant Levels

Low refrigerant levels can significantly impact your AC’s cooling ability. If you suspect this might be the issue, it's crucial to contact a trusted HVAC specialist in Houston. Handling refrigerant requires specific tools and expertise.

Inspect the Outdoor Unit

Your AC’s outdoor unit needs to be free of debris and obstruction. Leaves, dirt, and other debris can accumulate and restrict airflow, affecting the cooling efficiency. Regularly inspect and clean your unit to maintain optimal performance.

When to Call a Professional

If you've gone through these steps and your AC is still not cooling effectively, it may be time to call in a professional. Persistent issues may indicate more serious problems like electrical issues, a failing compressor, or other mechanical malfunctions.
