HVAC Maintenance for Houston Humidity: What a Seasonal Visit Actually Covers

Living in Houston means dealing with high humidity levels almost year-round. This humidity can put a strain on your HVAC system, making regular maintenance essential. Understanding what a seasonal maintenance visit involves can help you keep your system in top shape.

Why Is HVAC Maintenance Important in Humid Climates?

Houston's humidity can lead to increased wear and tear on your HVAC system. Regular maintenance helps ensure your system runs efficiently, prevents unexpected breakdowns, and extends its lifespan. Additionally, a well-maintained system can improve indoor air quality by effectively managing moisture levels.

What Does a Seasonal Maintenance Visit Include?

During a seasonal maintenance visit, a technician from Love Air Conditioning Company will perform several crucial tasks:

  • Inspection and Cleaning: The technician will inspect all components of your HVAC system, including coils, filters, and ductwork, and clean any buildup that might impede performance.
  • System Testing: They will test the system to ensure it's operating as it should, checking for any signs of potential issues.
  • Refrigerant Levels: Ensuring proper refrigerant levels is vital for efficient cooling, especially in Houston's humid climate.
  • Thermostat Calibration: Accurate thermostat settings are crucial for maintaining comfortable indoor temperatures.
  • Lubrication of Moving Parts: This reduces friction, preventing wear and ensuring your system runs smoothly.

When to Call a Technician

If you notice uneven cooling, strange noises, or a spike in your energy bills, it's time to call a technician. Addressing issues early can prevent costly repairs or system failures.
