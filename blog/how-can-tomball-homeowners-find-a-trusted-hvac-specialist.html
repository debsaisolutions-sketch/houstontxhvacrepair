DIY Checks Before Calling a Specialist

Before you reach out to a professional, there are a few DIY steps you can take to troubleshoot common HVAC issues:

  • Check the Thermostat: Ensure it's set to the correct mode (heat or cool) and the temperature is properly adjusted.
  • Inspect Air Filters: Dirty filters can restrict airflow and reduce efficiency. Regularly replacing or cleaning them can often solve performance issues.
  • Examine Circuit Breakers: A tripped breaker could be the simple reason your system isn't running. Reset it and see if that resolves the issue.
