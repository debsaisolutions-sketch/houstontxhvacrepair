Common Signs Your AC Needs Professional Attention

Inconsistent Cooling

If you notice uneven cooling throughout your home, it could be a sign of a more significant issue. Whether certain rooms are warmer than others or the system cycles on and off frequently, these inconsistencies often require a technician's expertise.

Unusual Noises

While some noise is normal, strange sounds like grinding, squealing, or banging should not be ignored. These noises can indicate problems like loose components or failing parts, which need professional assessment.

Foul Odors

Musty or burning smells coming from your AC are red flags. These odors could originate from mold in the ductwork or burnt-out wires, both of which require immediate attention from an expert.

Frequent Resets

If you find yourself resetting your AC often, it may be a sign of an underlying problem. Continuous resets can strain your system and lead to more severe issues down the line.
